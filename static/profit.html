Triple-barrier replay (López de Prado, Advances in Financial Machine Learning, 2018): every past bar becomes a trade entered on the next bar's open, closed at the target (1.5 × ATR), the stop (1 × ATR, assumed first when one bar touches both), 16 bars or 15:10 IST, net of your broker's charges and slippage. Logistic regression + gradient boosting, validated walk-forward on bars they never trained on. Research only, not investment advice.
